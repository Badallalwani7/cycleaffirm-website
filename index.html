How it works

Four phases, four moods. One line a day that gets it.

  1. Log your period

    Tap “Period started?” when it does. CycleAffirm works out your phase and day and shows them on a ring.

  2. See what's coming

    “Period in about 6 days.” Every prediction is labelled an estimate, and it learns your real cycle length as you log.

  3. Get a line for the phase

    Each morning there's an affirmation written for where you are, in the voice of a friend who's funny about it and kind about it.

Try a check-in

How are you, really?

In the app, a check-in takes seconds. Tell it how you actually feel and today's line changes to match you, not just the day of your cycle. Try the mood part here.

  • A few sliders, a few words. Energy, mood, sleep, calm, body and self-worth, the moods that fit, and a note if you want one.
  • A line that fits. You get a balance score and the best line for your phase and mood. Add a note, or have a really low day, and the AI writes you a fresh one, if you've said yes to AI.
  • It learns what helps. Check in a few times and, when a phase ends, it remembers what worked, so that phase's lines fit better next cycle.

Pinky promises

Your cycle is not our business model.

Period data is personal. Here's the short version. The privacy policy has every detail.

  • Stored with Firebase

    Your data lives in Google's Firebase, behind rules that let only you read it. People you invite see only what you chose to share.

  • AI only after “I agree”

    Affirmations are written with help from Anthropic's AI. Nothing goes to it until you tap “I agree”, your email and account ID never do, and you can switch it off in Settings.

  • Never sold. No ads.

    No ads, no ad SDKs, and no tracking you across other apps. This website has no trackers either.

  • Delete it all, anytime

    One tap in the app deletes your account and data. No app any more? Ask us on the web.
