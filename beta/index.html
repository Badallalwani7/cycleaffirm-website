Get your invite.

CycleAffirm is a period tracker and an affirmation app in one, and it's in invite-only testing on Google Play. Here's how to get in.

How it works

  1. Request an invite below. Use the Google account you sign in to Google Play with, since that's the one we add.
  2. We add you to the testers list as spots open, and email you a link.
  3. Open the link on your Android phone, tap to join the test, then install CycleAffirm from Google Play like any other app.

Who it's for

People 18 and over with an Android phone. There's no iPhone version yet.

What it isn't

It isn't birth control, a fertility tool or medical advice. Predictions are estimates.
