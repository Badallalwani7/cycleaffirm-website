Get your invite.

CycleAffirm is a period tracker and an affirmation app in one, and it's in invite-only testing on Google Play. Here's how to get in.

How it works

  1. Join the beta list below. Use the Google account you sign in to Google Play with.
  2. Join the testers group when the next screen asks. It's one tap, with the same Google account.
  3. Open the Google Play link on your Android phone, tap “Become a tester”, then install CycleAffirm like any other app.

About your details

When you join the beta list, we keep your first name, your email, and the time you signed up, in a private Google Sheet that only the CycleAffirm team can open. We use them to invite you to the beta and to tell you when CycleAffirm launches. Nothing else: never sold, never used for ads. Want off the list? Email badallalwani@scrolltrace.in and we'll delete your details. The bloom quiz on the home page runs only in your browser, so your answers are never sent anywhere.

Who it's for

People 18 and over with an Android phone. There's no iPhone version yet.

What it isn't

It isn't birth control, a fertility tool or medical advice. Predictions are estimates.
